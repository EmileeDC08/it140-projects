## Theme and Storyline

School-theme

In this game, you play an eager student desperate to leave the last day of school and enjoy summer-break. Unfortunately, you have one more assignment to turn in before you can leave. Miss Stricker has locked all the doors and the only escape is through her class. You must explore all rooms and find all 6 items to finish the assignment and turn in! If you make it to her room without all items, you will have 6 weeks of summer school! If you find all items and turn in the assignment, you get to enjoy your summer!
